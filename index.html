ISA - Tax-Free

All growth, dividends and withdrawals are completely tax-free. You never pay capital gains tax, dividend tax or income tax on what you take out.

Pension - Tax-Deferred

Growth inside a pension is tax-free, but withdrawals are taxed as income. You get 25% tax-free (up to £268,275 lifetime), with the rest taxed at your marginal rate.

GIA - Taxable

No tax advantages. Gains above the £3,000 CGT exemption are taxed at 18% (basic) or 24% (higher rate). Dividends above £500 are also taxable. Best used once ISA and pension allowances are maxed out.
